# The news of Monday 23 November 2026

The five stories on the front page, each checked against reporting from
23 November 2026. All five happened, or were first reported, on that day.

## 1. COP31 collapses — climate talks fail

The UN climate talks at COP31 ended without a deal. The EU and other
delegations rejected any agreement that had no language on phasing out
fossil fuels, so the negotiations collapsed over fossil-fuel transition
plans and carbon-removal technologies.

Sources: The Hindu (via Newsmriti's 23 Nov headlines); FeedCast Daily
Brief, Monday 23 November 2026.

## 2. Trump, Putin and Xi meet in Shenzhen

Donald Trump, Vladimir Putin and Xi Jinping held a trilateral summit on
the sidelines of APEC in Shenzhen, China, and issued a "Declaration
toward Peace". On the same day the US–Iran confrontation sharpened:
President Trump threatened to sink Iranian vessels after an American
strike on an oil tanker, and Houthi forces claimed strikes on a Saudi
oil refinery and radar sites.

Sources: SimpleLife.news, Daily News 23 November 2026; TALi News,
World News 23 November 2026; FeedCast Daily Brief, 23 November 2026.

## 3. AI selloff rocks markets

A global AI-driven selloff shook Asian bourses. South Korea's KOSPI
dropped about 5% before finding support, and the finance minister
pledged market-stabilisation measures; Taiwan stocks swung nearly
1,600 points intraday before TSMC turned higher. US futures rebounded
afterwards. In the same session South Korea's customs service said the
country's annual exports had passed $1 trillion for the first time,
driven by AI-related chip demand — the fourth economy ever to do so.

Sources: SimpleLife.news, Daily News 23 November 2026; FeedCast Daily
Brief, 23 November 2026.

## 4. Russian warship sunk off Benghazi

The Russian frigate Merkury sank in Benghazi after a suspected
Ukrainian naval drone attack, according to Libyan officials. Russian
strikes on Kyiv continued, collapsing a residential building as
residents searched for missing people.

Sources: SimpleLife.news, Daily News 23 November 2026; Newsmriti's
23 Nov headlines (The Hindu and others); HuddleWorld headlines.

## 5. Riemann Hypothesis claimed proved

Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge and Claude claim to have proved
the Riemann Hypothesis, a problem unresolved for over 160 years.

Sources: SimpleLife.news, Daily News 23 November 2026 (top stories).
